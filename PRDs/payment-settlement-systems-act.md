**Audience:** developers and agents adding the Payment and Settlement Systems Act case type

A complaint under Section 25 of the Payment and Settlement Systems Act, 2007 (PSS Act) is
filed the same way as a complaint under Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act. Only
the instrument changes: a debit mandate and a failed debit take the place of the cheque
and the cheque return memo.

**How this document relates to [`efiling.md`](efiling.md).** Everything in `efiling.md`
applies to a PSS Act case, except the sections this document writes out. A section that
is exactly the same for a PSS Act case is listed in §1 and not repeated. A section with
any change is written out here in full, and for a PSS Act case this document's version is
the one that applies. A field that is the same as in `efiling.md` keeps its ID there
(`LDN-3`); a field that exists only for a PSS Act case has a new ID (`MND-1`, `DBT-1`, or
the section's prefix with `P`, such as `DCM-P1`).
