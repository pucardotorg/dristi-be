# Case Numbers

**Status:** 2026-10-19 — case numbers only; application, evidence, witness and access-code
numbers removed for now. Kerala: every number generated by DRISTI — filing number at draft,
CMP and CNR at registration, ST at cognizance, LP when the case moves to the long pending
register. Gujarat: filled from the Case Numbering System sheet, plus a DRISTI pre-filing
number. Punjab & Haryana: NACT filing number, CNR and case number from the same sheet.
Earlier drafts: 2026-09-09, 2026-09-15.

**Date:** 2026-10-19

Format, timing and generator all differ by state, so each state has its own table. **Generated
by** is either DRISTI or CIS. `?` means not known yet. Where a state registers a case as one
of several types, the type is chosen at Scrutiny and Registration —
[`scrutiny-registration-handover.md`](scrutiny-registration-handover.md) `SCR-13`.

## 1. Kerala

| # | Number | When it is generated | Format | Example | Generated by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Filing number | On draft creation | `[tenantId]-[6-digit sequence]-[year]` | `KL-000049-2025` | DRISTI |
| 2 | CMP number (Criminal Miscellaneous Petition) | At registration | `CMP/[sequence]/[year]` | `CMP/58/2025` | DRISTI |
| 3 | CNR (Case Number Record) | At registration | `[courtId][6-digit sequence][year]` — 16 characters | `KLKM520000232025` | DRISTI |
| 4 | ST number | At cognizance | `ST/[sequence]/[year]` | `ST/6/2025` | DRISTI |
| 5 | LP number (Long Pending) | When the case moves to the long pending register | `LP/[sequence]/[year]` | `LP/29/2025` | DRISTI |

## 2. Gujarat

Gujarat registers a case as one of three types, each with its own series: **eCC** Criminal
Case, **eCR EN** Criminal Inquiry Case, **eCR MA** Criminal Miscellaneous Application.

| # | Number | When it is generated | Format | Example | Generated by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pre-filing number | On draft creation | ? | ? | DRISTI |
| 2 | Filing number | On completion of scrutiny, when the case is classified as one of the three types | `[type]/[sequence]/[year]` — next sequence in the Filing registry for that type | `eCC/7/2026` · `eCR EN/7/2026` · `eCR MA/7/2026` | CIS |
| 3 | CNR (Case Number Record) | With the filing number; unchanged thereafter — through reclassification, registration, cognizance and disposal | `GJAH` + 2-digit court ID + 6-digit sequence + 4-digit year | `GJAH010001252026` | CIS |
| 4 | Registration number | At registration. The type is usually kept but may be changed to either of the others. A fresh sequence; the filing sequence is not carried forward | `[type]/[sequence]/[year]` — next sequence in the Registration registry for that type | `eCC/11/2026` · `eCR EN/11/2026` · `eCR MA/11/2026` | CIS |
| 5 | Case number | Only where cognizance is taken; every registered type becomes eCC. Where cognizance is not taken, the matter is disposed of and no case number is allotted | `eCC/[sequence]/[year]` — next sequence in the Case registry for eCC | `eCC/3/2026` | CIS |

## 3. Punjab & Haryana

A §138 case is registered as **NACT** (Negotiable Instruments Act).

| # | Number | When it is generated | Format | Example | Generated by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Filing number | When the case file is submitted at the filing branch | `NACT/[sequence]/[year]` | `NACT/9642/2026` | DRISTI |
| 2 | CNR (Case Number Record) | When the case file is submitted at the filing branch | Panchkula Special NI Act Court: `HRPK03` + 6-digit sequence + year | `HRPK030096462026` | CIS |
| 3 | Case number (registration number) | At registration | `NACT/[sequence]/[year]` | `NACT/566/2026` | CIS |
