Process lifecycle

How a notice, summons, warrant, proclamation, attachment or custom process moves from the judge's order to a recorded outcome. Everything after step 2 runs separately for each process.

System done by the system Manual done by a person Status process status after the step Task citizen pending task created at this step
1

Judge issues the order

Judge, on the order screen · PRC-01, PRC-03

Judge enters, per recipient

  • The recipient. A company and each person in charge are separate options PRC-02
  • One or more channels, from those configured for the deployment PRC-04
  • For a police channel, the police station

System fills in System

  • Party taking steps: accused → complainant; complainant → accused; witness → their side AUT-02
  • Police station, from the recipient's address and the police-station master AUT-04, PRC-08
  • If it can't tell (e.g. a court witness), the judge chooses AUT-03
2

One process per recipient, per channel, per address

System · PRC-02, §3

Summons order ├── Accused A · RPAD · home address → process 1 ├── Accused A · RPAD · office address → process 2 ├── Accused A · SMS · mobile → process 3 └── Accused B · Police (NSTEP) · station → process 4

Each process has its own fee, envelope, signature and status. From here on, nothing waits on another process.

3

Prerequisites, as pending tasks on the party taking steps

Party taking steps; court staff record the envelope · §7, §8

Court fee

Flat fee per process type. Drawn from the case's single pool of court fee already paid; only the balance is asked for. Excess stays in the pool.

FEE-01, FEE-04–FEE-07

Channel fee

e-Post only, from a fee service. Not at launch.

FEE-02, FEE-03

Envelope Manual

RPAD and Police (RPAD) only. One envelope per process, handed in at the counter; court staff record receipt.

STP-01–STP-03

The process can't be signed until its own fees are paid and its own envelope is received. If the expiry date passes first, it expires unsent.

Status: Pending
4

Sign

Warrant

CMO

Notice, summons, custom process. Proclamation and attachment to be confirmed (Q-11).

Each process is signed separately, even when several are selected and signed in one click. Signing does not send.

A warrant does not print the hearing date. It carries a QR code and a line telling the reader to scan it for the latest hearing date. PRC-06

5

Send

System or a person, set per channel · DSP-01–DSP-03

SMS, Email, Police (NSTEP), Police (iCOPS)

Manual marked sent

6

Update status

A separate step from send, and set separately per channel · DSP-01, DSP-08, DSP-09

System reported back

SMS gateway receipt · Email (Q-9) · NSTEP via CIS poll · iCOPS callback

Manual recorded

CMO from the acknowledgement card or the station's report; Post Officer for e-Post.

Every outcome carries a comment and an optional file. A negative outcome also needs a reason from the state's list.

Court side, manual channels: listed at Sent until the outcome is recorded.

Outcome: Delivered / Executed Not delivered / Not executed Failed
7

Process closes

On its outcome, or on the expiry date, whichever comes first · DSP-06, EXP-01–EXP-03

At any point before an outcome

Expired The expiry date passed. Open pending tasks close with it.

Recalled Warrant recalled or bail granted: every process for that recipient in the round is recalled. Where one was already sent by hand, the CMO records that the recall was communicated PIA-01

Hearing date changes: nothing happens to the warrant. It prints no date, so nothing on it is wrong; the QR code shows the new date. No new document, signature or dispatch. PIA-02

Not delivered never creates a new process. A new attempt needs a new order and a new round. DSP-07

Pending tasks

Pending tasks are citizen-side only. Court-side work — sign, mark sent, record outcome, record a recall — is a list of processes on the Sign process screen, segregated by status. SGN-02
